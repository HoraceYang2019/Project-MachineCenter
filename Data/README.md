The dataset provides the associated files for machining guidance. The question lists are used to guide the selection of feasible machining parameters after reviewing the machining history.

1. Manual (pdf): <br>
   An operation manual for the smart tool holder of CNC machining <br>

3. Machining Resume (yaml): <br>
   Production data covering 10 months, ranging from 202507 to 202605, at different sites 
   20250702_MIRDC,..., 20260427_TT

4. Reports (pdf): <br>
   Production reports by machining resume.
    
5. Data files (csv): <br>
   Collected data from the smart tool holders and the CNC controllers by time and coordinates.

6. Question List (csv):<br>
   a. Independent Instruction Questions (pdf only).csv <br>
   b. Continuous Instruction Questions (pdf only).csv <br>
   c. Cross-Source Instruction Questions (pdf+csv).csv <br>
   d. Continuous and Cross-Source Instruction (pdf+csv+report).csv
